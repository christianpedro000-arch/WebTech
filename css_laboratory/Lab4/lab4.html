Content

The innermost area that holds the text and images.

Padding

The space between the content and the border.

Margin

The space outside the border that separates elements.

Reflection

Padding is the space inside an element, between its content and its border. Margin is the space outside the border that separates the element from other elements.

box-sizing: border-box makes the width and height include the padding and border, so a 280px card stays 280px wide. Without it, the padding and border are added on top of the width and the element becomes bigger than expected.

Excessive margin pushes elements far apart, wastes space, and can make items wrap or overflow on small screens, which makes the layout longer and harder to read.
