Hi! I’m Christian Jay B. Pedro, an aspiring web developer and a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student majoring in Web Development at the University of the Cordilleras. I was born on August 18, 2007, and I originally come from Tabio, Mankayan, Benguet. However, my decision to pursue this course opened the door to a new world of technology, creativity, and problem-solving. As I continue my studies, I am learning how websites work, how to write code, and how to transform ideas into functional and visually appealing digital experiences.

I believe that being a beginner is not a weakness but an opportunity to grow. Every challenge I encounter helps me gain new knowledge, improve my abilities, and become more confident in my chosen field.
